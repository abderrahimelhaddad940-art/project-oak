Common Estate Myths

Myth #1: "I have a Will, so I'm safe."

Reality: A Will is just a letter to a probate judge. It doesn't skip court; it's the ticket to enter it. Only a Trust keeps your family out of probate.

Myth #2: "Estate planning is only for the wealthy."

Reality: The wealthy use trusts to save taxes; middle-class families use them to save their heirs from $10,000+ in unnecessary legal fees and years of stress.

Myth #3: "I'm too young to worry about this."

Reality: If you have children or own a home, you are at the right age. If something happens today, who will raise your kids? Who manages your house?

Myth #4: "I'll just use a free template."

Reality: Estate laws vary by state. A generic 2-page template from the internet often lacks the specific legal language required by your local courts to be valid.

Myth #5: "It's too complicated and takes too long."

Reality: It used to be. With GuardTies, you can complete the work in a single weekend. We provide the roadmap; you just follow the steps.
