Common Estate Myths

Myth #1: "I have a will, so I'm covered."

Reality: A will doesn't skip probate — it's the document that starts it. Only a trust keeps your estate out of court.

Myth #2: "This is only for wealthy families."

Reality: The wealthy use trusts to save on taxes; everyone else uses them to save their family $10,000+ in legal fees and a year of stress.

Myth #3: "I'm too young for this."

Reality: If you have kids or own a home, you're not too young — you're exactly on time. Who raises your kids tomorrow if something happens tonight?

Myth #4: "A free template is good enough."

Reality: Estate law varies by state. Most free templates aren't written to meet any state's specific requirements — which is often what makes them unenforceable.

Myth #5: "It'll take forever."

Reality: Most people finish in a weekend. You get an 8-section roadmap that tells you exactly what to do, in order.
