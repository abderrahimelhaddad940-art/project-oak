Refund Policy

A straightforward 30-day guarantee. No questions asked.

Last updated: July 30, 2026

We want you to buy GuardTies with confidence. If it isn't everything we described, you get your money back — plain and simple.

1. The Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with the Product for any reason, you may request a full refund within 30 days of your original purchase date.

2. How to Request a Refund

Email support@guardties.com from the address used at checkout, with your order confirmation and the date of purchase. We don't require an explanation, but if something specifically didn't work for you, we'd genuinely like to hear it so we can improve the Product.

3. Processing

Refunds are issued to your original payment method. Once approved, refunds are typically processed within 5–10 business days, though your bank or card issuer may take additional time to post the credit to your account.

4. After 30 Days

Refund requests submitted more than 30 days after purchase are handled at our discretion. Email us and we'll take a look — extenuating circumstances are considered on a case-by-case basis.

5. Access After a Refund

Because the Product is a digital download, we ask that you discontinue use of the documents once a refund has been issued. Documents you have already signed, notarized, or filed remain valid as executed; the refund applies to the purchase price of the Product, not to any legal effect already given to a completed document.

The bottom line: if it isn't right for you, tell us within 30 days and we'll refund it — no forms, no runaround.

6. Contact

Questions about a refund can be sent any time to support@guardties.com.
